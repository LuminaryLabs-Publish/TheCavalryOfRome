Command a living landscape

The 3D hexes conform to the world terrain. Real river crossings require a bridge; forests and rocks block movement. Pick a card from your hand, then press PLAY CARD. Only formations authorized by that card can receive orders.

Click a gold-ringed Roman miniature, then a green hex to move or a red-ringed enemy to attack. Drag to orbit the map; scroll to zoom. Each ordered formation may move and attack once, if its card allows both. Unit pedestals distinguish factions, and each formation shows soldier count and health.

Press END COMMAND to resolve the enemy's response and draw a new card. Keyboard: 1–5 select card, Enter play/end, Esc clear selection.
